# Rohingya Development Organisation (RDO)
### *Educate • Empower • Build Tomorrow*

Official repository and website for the **Rohingya Development Organisation (RDO)**, a community-based, non-profit humanitarian initiative founded in Cox's Bazar, Bangladesh.

> *“Every child has a dream. Every young person has potential. Displacement should not destroy their future.”*

---

## 🌟 About RDO

The Rohingya Development Organisation (RDO) works with humility, transparency, and deep respect for the community to provide education, digital literacy, life skills, child protection, and psychosocial opportunities for displaced Rohingya children and youth.

## 🏫 Flagship Project: RDO Learning Centres

- **Target Population**: 200 Rohingya children and youth across 4 camps
- **Centres**: 4 community-based learning centres (50 students each; Camps 4, 5, 17 & 18)
- **Gender Target**: At least 50% girls enrolled
- **Schedule**: 08:30 – 15:00 daily structured shifts
- **Approach**: Complementary community-led initiative coordinated with camp authorities

### Core Pillars
1. **Foundational Learning**: Literacy, numeracy, English, and study clubs
2. **Digital Literacy**: Basic computer operation, typing, and cyber safety
3. **Life Skills & Leadership**: Decision making, teamwork, and financial literacy
4. **Child Protection**: Safeguarding, safe help-seeking, and exploitation prevention
5. **Gender Equality & GBV Prevention**: Mutual respect, body boundaries, and safe referrals
6. **Sports & Creative Arts**: Football, cricket, drawing, storytelling, and trauma healing
