Who We Are

Rohingya Development Organisation

RDO is a community-based, non-profit organisation founded in Cox's Bazar, Bangladesh. We work with humility, transparency, and deep respect for our community to restore hope and dignity to displaced children and youth.

Our Vision

To build a safer, educated, skilled and hopeful future for Rohingya children and young people, equipping them to overcome displacement and contribute positively to society.

Our Mission

To support vulnerable Rohingya children and youth through educational support, learning opportunities, life skills, child-protection awareness, and developmental activities that nurture their innate human potential.

Complementary Approach

Complements, does not duplicate, existing formal aid.

Strict Safeguarding

100% trained staff in PSEA and child protection.

Gender Equality Focus

At least 50% target for girls in all cohorts.

Full Accountability

Transparent budget, quarterly reviews & MEAL.

Flagship Initiative

RDO Learning Centres for Children & Youth

A 12-month community-based project establishing 4 safe learning spaces serving 200 Rohingya children and adolescent youth in Camps 4, 5, 17, and 18 in Cox's Bazar, Bangladesh.

200 Direct Learners

50 Students per Camp Centre

Organised in two competency groups of about 25 students each per centre. Alternating morning and afternoon shifts allow optimal use of space and resources at minimal cost.

08:30 – 15:00 Schedule

Structured Daily Programming

Covers literacy, numeracy, English communication, study support, basic computer operations, life skills, GBV prevention sessions, team sports, and healing arts.

$250 Per Student / Year

Total Budget: USD $50,000

High-impact, low-overhead model where 38% goes straight to education facilitators and teachers. Fully audited with MEAL compliance and transparent tracking.

How Each Learning Centre Operates

Designed for maximum safety, cultural alignment, child protection, and community trust.

Step 1

Community Mapping

Coordination with camp authorities, RRRC, UNHCR, and NRC before any activity. Verified gap mapping.

Step 2

Safe Spaces

Approved community structures equipped with desks, whiteboards, digital kits, and clean WASH facilities.

Step 3

Trained Facilitators

2 facilitators per centre (at least 1 woman), screened under child safeguarding and PSEA protocols.

Step 4

Survivor Referrals

Confidential complaint channels with mapped referral pathways to certified clinical and protection actors.

Holistic Development

The Six Core Pillars of Learning

Every child receives balanced cognitive, digital, emotional, and social development delivered in a safe, culturally sensitive environment.

1. Foundational Learning

Literacy, Numeracy & English Clubs

  • Basic and functional literacy and numeracy support
  • Spoken English and communication skills
  • Reading clubs, homework assistance and study habits
  • Interactive learning games and regular progress tests

2. Digital Literacy

Basic Computing & Cyber Safety

  • Computer fundamentals, keyboard typing & mouse control
  • Basic file management and document creation
  • Online safety, digital citizenship & scam awareness
  • Supervised responsible tech access for displaced youth

3. Life Skills & Leadership

Empowering Adolescent Agency

  • Interpersonal communication, active listening & empathy
  • Team problem solving and collaborative decision making
  • Youth leadership development and civic responsibility
  • Basic financial literacy, budgeting & career awareness

4. Child Protection & Rights

Safeguarding & Help-Seeking

  • Child rights education tailored to refugee context
  • Safe disclosure training and confident help-seeking paths
  • Prevention of child marriage, child labor & exploitation
  • Confidential, child-friendly complaints and feedback box

5. Gender Equality & GBV

Mutual Respect & Safe Boundaries

  • Body safety, personal boundaries and mutual respect
  • Healthy relationships and peaceful conflict resolution
  • Separate sessions for adolescent girls and boys
  • Caregiver and community leader engagement dialogues

6. Sports, Arts & Recreation

Psychosocial Healing & Play

  • Organised football, cricket and physical fitness games
  • Drawing, painting, and visual storytelling workshops
  • Rohingya folklore, poetry and cultural expression
  • Trauma-informed psychosocial team-building exercises

Proactive Risk Management

Weather hazards (monsoons/cyclones) are mitigated through contingency scheduling. Access permissions are secured with RRRC and camp authorities before launch.

Survivor-Centred GBV Referral Protocols Strictly Enforced.
Protection & Accountability

Strict Child Safeguarding & PSEA Commitment

At RDO, the safety and dignity of every child is non-negotiable. Before any child-facing activity begins, all personnel must sign our Code of Conduct and undergo mandatory training.

Zero Tolerance for Exploitation & Abuse (PSEA)

Strict adherence to UN and international PSEA guidelines with safe screening and vetting during all recruitment.

Confidential Complaints Channels

Child-friendly and caregiver-friendly feedback boxes in every centre with direct oversight by our Protection Focal Point.

Survivor-Centred Mapped Referral Pathways

RDO does not conduct clinical or unauthorized GBV case handling; disclosures are referred immediately to qualified humanitarian health partners.

Project Staffing & Governance Plan

Project Manager & Education Coordinator
Implementation, coordination with NRC/camp authorities, reporting, and pedagogy quality.
8 Centre Facilitators / Teachers (2 per centre)
Community educators. At least 1 woman per centre where feasible, delivering daily sessions.
Safeguarding, GBV & Protection Focal Point (Part-time)
Female focal point managing confidential complaints, safe disclosures, and survivor referral pathways.
MEAL & Finance / Admin Assistant (Part-time)
Monitoring data verification, feedback records, procurement transparency, and accounting.
Supervised Community Volunteers
Parent and youth volunteers supporting sports, art exhibitions, and community consultation.
